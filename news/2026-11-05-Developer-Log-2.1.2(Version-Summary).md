Another minor content update has gone live yesterday. Once again without a dungeon rework, but with plenty of new content nonetheless.

I opted to prioritize actual database work this time around, so 2.1.1 only received two spell patches. 2.1.1a and 2.1.1b improved some consumables (mostly alcohols and NPC-crafted consumables) and some profession recipes that slipped through the cracks. Some minor changes were introduced to a few lua scripts in order to fix bugs and improve the overall leveling experience.

As far as new content goes - 155 (of some 350) rare spawn NPCs were buffed and given new movesets. They are now functionally overworld minibosses and can start hitting quite hard by the midgame. This was the first of three batches I plan on doing; the remainder will be taken care of in updates 2.1.4 and 2.1.5.

A new variant of player companions was added, 5 in total, in the same race/class restrictive fashion as the original ones. Unlike them, these new variants do not have the charge -> AoE type ability, but instead a ranged AoE attack. This is an advantage in some encounters, but suboptimal in others.

Glyphs were entirely removed from the game. I dedicated three or four massive patches up until now solely aimed at balancing them and making them a bit more special to the experience, but I have given up completely and don't plan on making another attempt at it ever again. Inscription will get new uses however in the form of offensive scrolls. Two were implemented and I plan on adding more once I figure out what I want. I am also considering making Inscription "Blacksmithing for casters", but I still need to figure out how to go about it exactly.

The Hardcore system took a bit of a hit due to some buggy behavior. I had to disable the objectives tied to hardcore - so no more mandatory dungeon delving in order to advance in levels for the time being. However, Hardcore players now gain two epic mounts immediately at level 1 to speed up their inevitable death progress. They also receive a fancy new patron-voted title "the Unyielding".

Classes and builds did not experience that many changes, other than Protection Warriors and Restoration Shamans. Protection received some serious rebalancing to tone down its damage and the tree has been reworked the leveling experience is a bit smoother. Restoration received some talent rebalancing and spell changes to make leveling smoother as well, but not to the extent Protection received.

And last but not least - 7 new murderbots have joined the roster, 5 of which were designed by patrons. The murdersquad is filling out nicely, as is their coverage of the overworld.

Other than that, 2.1.2 brought plenty of bugfixes, script/npc/spell/item tweaks and miscellaneous changes, which I won't be listing here as they are mostly very random, minor edits. If you want to read the full devlog, it is included in the patch zip.

That is all for now. A big thank you to all of my patrons. You guys keep me motivated and your donations that have been piling up are going to become an 11/10 present for my boys come December, for which I am deeply grateful to all of you.

Stay tuned for 2.1.3, which will bring a reworked Blackrock Depths (first half) and probably much more on top of that.

The next public release won't be until 2.1.5 (in around a year or more), so if you aren't a patron yet - consider donating for access to updated content and regular minor updates. I have started using the Patron-Discord integration bot, so you don't have to wait around for me to send you links manually anymore.

As always - happy gaming, and see you around.
