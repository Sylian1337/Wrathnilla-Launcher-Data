I am happy to report that the first minor content update was released yesterday. While it brought no new dungeon reworks, Wrathnilla has progress significantly since it's 2.1 release.

I hope you will forgive me for speaking in broad terms and not giving too many details, but the patch and update notes are extensive and I don't want to clutter these posts with more than most people would be willing to read (and I may or may not have lost some of the patch notes by carelessly deleting older patch zips).

Patches 2.1.a through 2.1.k were focused largely on spell and talent balance, with a bit of item tweaking mixed in, and I feel that much was achieved. Some ridiculously overpowered things (for example Warrior's Revenge and related Protection talents) were toned down, while other crippling features (for example the healing negation brought on by the Paladin's Forbearance) were improved upon. The tailoring/leatherworking/blacksmithing grinds were also greatly reduced by lowering the material costs of items by 50-75% on average. Another great addition was the SoloCompanionScaling lua script, which greatly improves the leveling experience for players using a pet by increasing overworld mob difficulty as long as the player's companion is active.

Some significant redesigns happened as well, the most important one being that the new Evade ability now allows players to dodge single-target spells. All in all - hundreds of major and minor spell and talent edits.

Update 2.1.1 brought some overworld and dungeon-related bugfixes, and allowed me to correct some oversights in general. The dungeon content I had already reworked received additional attention as well (for example, some dungeon mobs gained the ability to see through stealth and invisibility, and the movement speed of bosses was increased to prevent cheesing via kiting). Some much needed QoL changes were also implemented, most importantly a 2-4 slot increase in non-crafted bags, introduction of crafting material bags added by the expansions to vanilla merchants, and a flat 5 gold cost cap to additional bank bag slots.

And last but not least, 7 new murderbots have joined the roster, 5 of which were designed by Patrons. Thank you for your ideas and designs guys, it was fun making them a reality. I am especially happy with the way the exploding mechanical squirrels turned out.

Speaking of Patrons, they have also cast their votes to determine the release order of upcoming dungeon reworks:
2.1.3 - first half of Blackrock Depths
2.1.6 - Lower Blackrock Spire
2.1.9 - Dire Maul East

I don't know what exactly 2.1.2 will bring, but I do know it will include a thorough rework of rare spawn creatures. I have been putting that off for long enough.

That is all for now, thank you for reading. Please consider becoming a Patron if you want access to all the updates and improvements before the big 2.1.5 public release goes live.
